## Routes, the severed rune and the pack (v17)
Asked for by playing it: the worker golem's logic was written when a rune was the only way between two areas. Since then the surface stair can be dug out and a road runs to Westwake, and a worker could use neither. The same history hid a bug in the rune network, and a gather order had nowhere to put things before the first chest. `tests/routes.test.ts` and `tests/runeHost.test.ts` hold all of it.

### The rune network's two ends agree

- **What was wrong.** `RuneLink.awakens` ("arriving wakes a dormant destination", for the first crossing, where the surface rune mends itself) was read in both directions. Take a glyph out of the halls' circle and the circle goes dark (`sim/ritual.ts: deactivate` clears `rune.dungeon.active`); but the surface rune kept its own flag, and a trip *down* through it "woke" the circle again, setting its flag over a ritual that was not active. Before the stair there was no way to stand on the surface with the circle broken, so it never showed.
- **`awakens` runs a -> b only.** `content/runes.ts: runeDestinations` returns `RuneHop`s (`node`, `link`, `wakes`); `wakes` is true only for the a -> b direction of an `awakens` link. `a` is lit by whatever lights it (the circle by its ritual) and by nothing else.
- **A rune has a state, derived and never stored** (`sim/runes.ts: runeState`): `dormant` (its own flag unset), `severed` (lit, but no far end answers: none is lit and it cannot wake one), `linked` (lit, with somewhere to go). `runeAnswers` is the one test of a far end, read by `travel` (which now refuses before it sets anything or instantiates the far area, and with several links takes the first that answers), by `runeWays` / `runeRoute`, and by the interaction. The surface rune keeps `rune.surface.active` for good (the anchor was rewritten); whether it carries follows the circle, so the two cannot get out of step and there was no field to add.
- **E on a rune that will not carry** picks `RULES.runeDormant` or the new `RULES.runeSevered` (`sim/interact.ts`), both data: a short reach whose `message` effect carries the new cue `runeDud`. Nothing is channelled, no `teleport` event is emitted and no flag is set.
- **How it looks** (`view/runeFx.ts`, pure functions of time; `game.ts: surfaceRuneState`, `runeRefused`). Left alone, a linked rune keeps its pulse (`linkedGlow`, the old constants) with motes rising; a severed one is carved but burns as embers (`severedGlow`, about a sixth of it, no motes, its light all but out), so it reads as unusable before anyone touches it. On `runeDud` the host sets `runeDud = {x, y, t}`: the fizzle, a jolt, sparks off the ring, and `runeDudGlow(t, rest, strength)` rides over the resting glow for `RUNE_DUD.dur` (1.15 s). The refusal is beats, as data (`RUNE_DUD`): a surge past full (the carvings drawn overdriven, as the arrival's pulse is), five flashes, each weaker than the last and unevenly spaced, with the rune dark between them, then one last breath that dies back to rest. The rune's light follows the same number. (The first version drew the stutter from two sines. Rendered beat by beat it was dark for most of the guttering and read as one flash and nothing, while a test that only asked for some bright sample passed on the surge alone; so the flashes are a table, each at least two 60 Hz frames long, and the host test counts them frame by frame.) A dormant rune only spits (half strength, on what is left of its carvings). The halls' circle still flickers on `fizzT`, as it did for the old `dud`.

### Routes over runes and portals (`sim/routes.ts`)

- **Ways out of an area** are of two kinds: rune hops (`runeWays`: from a lit rune to a far end that answers; they take the `travel` capability) and open portals (`portalOpen`; only `move`, since it is just walking). `firstHops(w, means, from, to)` is a breadth-first search over areas and returns every way to set out along a route of the fewest hops, in a stable order (runes, then portals, by id). It is derived every time: an order still saves only where it is going.
- **Unvisited areas are read, never instantiated.** A visited area gives its live portals; one nobody has entered is read once from its pure builder (`areaBase`, memoised per builder, as the debug menu reads a start), so a route can run through it and listing it changes nothing in the world. The area comes into being when a body walks in (`walkThrough` -> `getArea`).
- **Taking a hop** (`sim/jobs.ts: approach`). A rune: walk within 15 px, channel, `travel` (unchanged). A portal: `portalStand` picks a place inside its box where that body can stand (the middle first; every candidate lies well inside, so a walker that stops a pixel short still has its feet in it), the body walks there, and with `inPortal` true it calls the player's own `walkThrough`. A failed arrival is `arrivalBlocked`, as for a rune.
- **Which way, when there are two.** The halls and the surface are joined by the rune and by the stair. A body takes the nearest way (straight line from where it stands) that it has a path to, and then keeps to it: the way it is walking to is recognised by its saved walk goal (`Job.nav.goal`), so nothing new is stored, a save mid-walk carries on, and it cannot dither between two ways as it rounds a wall. With the rune dark only the stair is offered, and the other way about.
- **What the order screen offers** (`sim/orders.ts`). `canReach` is `routeExists`, so chests, stations and gather sources beyond a road or the stair are listed like those beyond a rune (only where the golem has been: `knownAt`). GO TO lists `knownAreas`: those the golem has been in, and those an open road or door leads to from a place the golem has been, by their start. Since v22 "has been" is a saved record (`world/discovery.ts`, [overworld.md](../world/overworld.md#discovery-what-the-golem-knows-v22)), not "the area is loaded": Westwake is offered once the golem has walked into it, and its shops with it (their doors stand in its zone); the far end of a rune is not known until it has been used, which keeps a worker from making the first crossing before the golem does. The block reason is `noRoute` ("NO WAY THERE"); `noTravel` remains for a body with no way at all and no `travel`.
- **Heard, not shown.** A worker's `portal` event is not the player's: `Game.workerWalked` plays the latch or a footfall where it left or came out, if that is where the player is, and touches neither the view, the door fade nor the note.

### Gathering to the pack

A gather order's `storage` may be `null`: the worker keeps what it gathers (`gatherOrder(resource, from, null, amount)`). The handler has no deposit phase then; the `harvest` effect counts what each blow put in the pack (`sim/tasks.ts`, at the fire point, as `deposit` counts where it lands, so a save between the blow and the end of the swing never counts twice), never past the amount asked for. It finishes where the work was, with what a last blow paid over the amount kept too; a full pack is `packFull`, as for any gather, and RETRY carries on once room is made (TRANSFER takes things out). On REPEAT it goes on while there is room. `gatherTargets` is the form's TO list: every chest the worker can get to, then `INVENTORY` (`ref: null`), which is always there, so the form can always be issued; with a chest built the default is still the chest.

### Saves (v17)

`SAVE_VERSION` is 17. No field changed shape. `MIGRATIONS[16]`: (1) a save in which the surface rune had woken a broken circle holds `rune.dungeon.active` over a ritual that is not active; the flag is cleared, so the circle is dark and the surface rune severed, as they now would be (a lit circle keeps its flag; names frozen in the migration). (2) A job stopped with the reason `noRuneRoute` carries `noRoute`.

### Tests

- `tests/routes.test.ts`: the severed rune through real interactions (a glyph taken out below; `travel` refused with nothing set; E giving the cue at the rune and its words; the glyph back and the rune carrying); the migration, and saves it must leave alone; a worker walking from its alcove up the stair and along the road into a Westwake nobody has visited, with the rune dark (and nothing offered while the stair is still buried); the nearer of rune and stair, with one walk goal all the way; every production portal (16) standable by a worker's body, walkable from its area's start and paired; a gather to the pack run to its amount across a save, blocked on a full pack, on repeat, and the TO list with and without a chest.
- `tests/runeHost.test.ts`: the refusal's beats (the surge, each flash weaker than the last and at least two frames long, dark between, the decay, determinism, the resting levels); the real host with keys (embers when severed, E playing the refusal with its flashes counted frame by frame, sparks and the light flaring, back to embers, nobody moved, nothing woken, and E carrying again once the circle is lit); a worker's portal event leaving the player's view, fade and note alone.
- Existing tests changed only where production did: the `SAVE_VERSION` pins (16 to 17) and the manage screen's "no chest" case (the form is now issued, to INVENTORY).

`node scripts/runePreview.mjs` (`tests/support/runeSnapshot.ts`) renders the rune left alone in its three states and a frame from each beat of a refusal, through the real host, to `.preview/rune/*.png`.

**Left alone on purpose.** `runeRoute` (runes alone) stays for its callers and tests. A worker does not try the other way after a failed arrival until its order is retried. Workers still cannot dig, so the stair has to be opened by the golem. Nothing in Westwake can be harvested, so it is a place to be sent, not yet a place to work.
