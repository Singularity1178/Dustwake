## Testing
`tests/monologue.test.ts` covers the monologue's sim side: line lint (font glyphs, width, known conditions), one-shot and cooldown on sim time, `when` / `if` gating, event triggers with field matching (other actors' events ignored), events from paused-world commands seen at drain, the `think` effect, `OBJECT ANALYZED.` through real scan mode, the shipped power lines through real interactions (E on the dormant golem; the guardian given the ruby core with C, then talking), save/load and determinism after a load, and the v10 to v11 migration. It also covers the Primer's single `learned` and its text, the intro script's data (lines, cues, ordering, original timings, diagnostics length, control after the last line), and the opening through the real host: the lines in order, no movement before control, control at `controlAt(INTRO)`, the list fading in play, R replaying the opening, a loaded save not replaying it. Existing tests changed only in their `SAVE_VERSION` pins (10 to 11); no golden hash changed.

The rollout suite (`tests/rollout.test.ts`) covers: the gated set, and that every gate has something to scan in a production area; the menu's reasons (what to scan, Z, within the panel; knowledge versus analysis); the taken-prop scan fix (the workbench after its chisel, the library table after its Primer; scanning never steals); dismantling reads a plan, so the only workbench can go and be rebuilt; the treasury chests teach the storage chest; the tutorial's critical path played through with nothing built, crafted or analyzed and no rule or story step reading a gate; the worker's spawn (id, determinism, a worker and not an NPC, no dialogue, violet) and the routes from both of its spots; C in and out on the golem and the guardian, powered-idle staying put, a full pack, and the one core cycling pack -> golem -> pack -> guardian -> pack -> golem across a save/load; both thoughts (never the dormant line about the guardian; the weak line in all five talk states, with the story unchanged); a fresh game reaching it all with no debug tools (the crown's route proven by flood fill: sealed until the cracked wall breaks); v4, v9, v10 and v11 to v12, with and without `know.build` (storage stays locked, exactly one worker, a player standing on its spot); the opening's framing; and the host's seams (core feedback, the management and storage notes). `tests/rubyArt.test.ts` pins the new art (both ruby icons, the worker body in every state) and, on rasterised passes, checks glow by state, that powered-idle differs from unpowered only at the core, and that the worker draws through `drawActor` into a vision-sized scratch canvas. Existing tests changed only where production did: the `SAVE_VERSION` pins (11 to 12); the crown's two goldens (with the old crown swapped back in, both lists reproduce their old hashes); the recipe set and the plank search; the "crafted material" set (inverse rows excluded, as in `rawValue`); the catalog's sprite check (a kind may borrow art); the guardian's reach check (it toggles only the guardian's body, now that a second NPC stands in the dungeon); the v9 fixture (no worker existed before v12); builders in the build and crafting tests that now also read every plan (`learnAllPlans`), and the knowledge-gating test's new analysis step. The old "planned lines" test is now the shipped-lines test.

The foundation suite adds `actorsStorage.test.ts`, `jobs.test.ts`, `analysisMigration.test.ts` and `foundationEdges.test.ts`: body/gear/capacity isolation, power compatibility versus sufficiency, generic storage and cache revisions, shared crafting contention and tools, inverse valuation and relic credit, finite harvest yields, cross-area gather/deposit and station orders, saves while walking/channeling/working and after effects fire, narrow-corridor yielding and bounded failure, scan routing, old v1/v9 saves, and a full job with DOM and `Math.random` access made to throw. Host tests also verify the scan key, nearby-world versus player-only event reactions, and that worker events cannot hijack the player's screens. Existing art golden files and the production builders' output are unchanged. The host is smaller after extracting actor drawing.

`npm test` compiles `tests/` and the DOM-free modules they import with the project's TypeScript (to CommonJS in `.test-out/`), then runs them on Node's built-in test runner. No new dependencies. The suite plays the whole tutorial headlessly (plus dialogue wrapping/pagination and full-registry art goldens) (the core now from the guardian) and covers change propagation, save round-trips, determinism, migrations (including v4 to v5 both ways), queued work by a non-player actor, sim-time scheduling, travel, the guardian encounter (interrupted and resumed, full pack, reachability, collision), a data-only second NPC, dialogue text lint, the zoom model and the guardian's sprite states, gear (basket pickup without ceremony, wear / take off and capacity, the fit check with stacking and the basket's own slot, the repack, save/load and the v7 migration, a second actor wearing gear), the slots (`tests/slots.test.ts`: which are usable and locked, unlocking by flag, a future slot item as a data row only, relocking, the arm and core decisions, the debug kit, the pack screen's navigation and states), crafting (`tests/crafting.test.ts`: the task and effect, the carried-not-consumed chisel, re-validation, overflow, another actor, a save mid-work, the rebalanced recipes and menu, the v8 -> v9 migration), deconstruction of everything buildable (`tests/salvage.test.ts`: an audit of both builders, refunds, the critical-prop refusals, refunds that survive a rebalance), the runes (`tests/runes.test.ts`: the 2.5x speed through the sim, the glow through the light system with a lit-area comparison, effects only while worn, new runes as rows), their art (`tests/gearArt.test.ts`; the pre-existing item, prop and golem goldens are untouched, with the legacy item golden now iterating the frozen list of items that existed before), and golem sprite goldens (`tests/golemArt.test.ts`: every pre-existing pose is pinned to a hash captured before gear and the slam existed; the basket and the slam frames have their own), and the attack (Space with nothing in front; next to an interactable, where E and Space do different things; blocked while busy; breaking cracked walls with the iron arm at the impact frame and only holding without it; re-validation; a slam saved mid-swing). `tests/host.test.ts` drives the real `Game` on the fake canvas with key presses (update only, no render): on the play screen Space slams while E / Enter interact with the same target, and in the pack Space still confirms with no slam behind it.

`tests/discovery.test.ts` (v22) holds what the order screens offer against where the golem has been: a fresh crossing (no town, road or shop on offer; a chest or bench in an unseen place not listed), walking west (each zone known from the moment it is announced, shops by their doors, nothing inside them), a worker's travels revealing nothing, the save round trip, and what a v21 save counts as known, case by case. `tests/chunkView.test.ts` holds the background's chunk cache on a canvas that stores real pixels (`tests/support/rasterCanvas.ts`): identical frames to the old single canvas, bounded memory, no bake in `prepare` while walking, an 8192-tile area, and the spatial lookups against brute force. Existing tests changed where the rule did: the `SAVE_VERSION` pins (21 to 22); tests that sent a worker somewhere the golem had never been now say the golem has been there (`discover`); the two perf reports and the lamp-hour host test read the view as it now is (no whole-area bake; the host keeps the lights near the view to the hour).
