# The stair below (v15, v16)

> Split out of the old single `ARCHITECTURE.md` (Phase 0.9). Index: [docs/index.md](../index.md).

## The stair below, with depth (v15)
Asked for by looking at it: render the stair below choked and cleared, and the stair head above, and compare (`node scripts/rockPreview.mjs stages above below`). Two things were wrong below, and both came from the same picture.

- **Three steps stood out in the Vestibule, cut off from the stair.** `stairfoot` was a prop: the flight's three lowest steps, narrower than the flight, set on the Vestibule's floor in front of the corridor. Between them and the flight proper lay the band of earth the fall had spilled, so it read as a scrap of stair lying on top of the dirt, the more so once the stair was dug out and nothing else was left there.
- **The flight was flat, and the head was not.** On the surface the open stair head has depth: each step lower is shallower, narrower and darker, between the well's two walls. Below, the same flight was six tiles of identical eight-pixel bands between two kerbs: a ladder laid on the floor.

### What it is now

**Data (`content/geography.ts`).** The way up is `GATE_PASSAGE` (the corridor plus the exit row: what the fall filled and what `ENTRANCES.surface_stair.below.clear` opens). It is two pieces: `GATE_HALL`, its two northern rows, a flagged hall out of the Vestibule (`FS.FLAG`), and `GATE_STAIR`, the four rows after it, the flight (`FS.STAIR`). Six tiles of steps were too long to give depth to: a flight that keeps widening for 96 px has either a foot too narrow to walk into or no visible taper, so the stair was shortened and what it no longer uses is the hall. `STAIR_CHEEK` holds the stonework's sizes (post 12 x 7, wall 8 wide) and how far the posts are raised off the flight (`post.lift`, 7: see "Post placement (v16)"), read by the builder, the prop rows and the tests. `STAIR_CHEEK_TOP` (`post.d - post.lift`) is where a post's feet stand and its cheek wall's footprint begins, in px south of the flight's north edge. The portal, its arrival point, the light and the `stairfoot` *spot* (a standing place for NPCs, in the Vestibule where the hall opens; it keeps its id) are where they were.

**No foot; earth instead.** The `stairfoot` prop, its `PROPS` row and its sprite are removed. Where it stood, the earth spill it used to cover is simply seen, with one more laid over it, and three more run on along the hall, thinning toward the flight, the last crumbs against the first step. They are bake decals, so they are there choked and cleared alike: dug out, the order met from the Vestibule is floor, dirt, flags showing through the dirt, first step. `stairfall` (the toe of the fall, which goes when the stair opens) is unchanged but for its words: nothing visible says "stair" from the Vestibule any more, so its examine text does (A HALL RUNS SOUTH FROM HERE TO THE SURFACE STAIR ...), as does the schematic's SURFACE STAIR.

**The flight has the head's depth (`art/stairs.ts`).** It is the same stair seen from the same place (south and above), so the same rule holds at both ends: a step that is higher is nearer the eye. The head counts down from the top and shrinks; the flight counts up from the floor and grows.
- `flightSteps(H)` lays a run `H` rows long out as steps, as data: about one to seven rows, the lowest five deep, each one up deeper (for the halls' 64 rows: nine steps of 5, 5, 6, 7, 7, 8, 8, 9, 9). Each has an `inset`, how far the wall's face comes in beside it: `KERB` beside the top step (the tread runs from coping to coping) and a pixel more for every step down, so the flight is 42 px wide at its foot and 58 at its top. Levels run from 1 to `TOP_LEVEL` (14, the step the head starts from), so `treadTone` makes the foot dark and the top light whatever the run's length.
- `flightPix(fx, fy, W, H)` draws, from each side inward: the **coping** (the old kerb: it does not step, and brightens with the row, not the step); the **wall's face** between coping and tread, a course to each step with a bed joint along the step's last row, the east face lit (`WALL_LIT`) with its foot catching the light and the west in its own shade (`WALL_SHADE`), throwing that shade along the tread ends, exactly as the well's walls are lit above; the **corner wedge** (`cornerShade`, at the step's own pitch); the **tread** (`stepShade` at the step's pitch, wear down the middle of the flight, one stone to a step, chips and pits as before).
- `tiles.ts: stairPix` now finds the run's south end as well as its north, since the layout depends on the run's length. Rubble on a step still does not end the run, and nothing it reads changes when the stair opens.

**The stonework stands in the way (`stairpost`, `staircheek`).** A wall face a body can walk onto is paint. `staircheek` is the cheek wall's footprint, one down each side of the flight: `flat`, no sprite (the wall is baked with the steps), a box 8 wide. That is never wider than the face drawn beside the lowest step it runs along (tested against `flightSteps`), so the golem stops against the wall, not short of it. Each ends at the foot of the flight in a `stairpost`: a real standing sprite, depth-sorted like any prop, a squared block of the stair's stone under a capstone (west) or broken off short when the vault came down (east). 40 px stay open between the posts. All four are placed by `buildDungeon` with fixed ids (`stairpost.w`, `staircheek.e`, ...), stand under the fall while the stair is choked (the one whole post's cap shows through the rubble), and are not things to use: no examine, no recipe, so nothing takes them apart.

### Saves

`SAVE_VERSION` is 16 (15 for the stair as first laid; v16 raises the posts, see below). No field changed shape (see "v15" under the save notes): styles are not in tile diffs, the rock piles kept their ids, and a save cannot hold state for the removed foot (it had none; one that names it anyway loads, tested). `MIGRATIONS[14]` only moves a body out of the new footprints, by the v14 rule and a frozen list of the four boxes and the passage's floor. `MIGRATIONS[15]` does the same for v16's boxes.

### Goldens and tests

Three hashes moved, each checked against the tree as it was. Sprite by sprite (pixel hash per kind, six variants, both states): no sprite that existed changed, the stair head included; `stairfoot` went and `stairpost` and `staircheek` came, which moves the registry's prop golden and `rockArt`'s stair golden (whose list named the foot). Tile by tile (both bakes on the pixel canvas): in the dungeon 32 tiles differ, all within 34-37, 48-55 (the passage's 24 and the earth at its mouth); on the surface none.

- `tests/rockArt.test.ts`: the layout (nine steps, end to end, never shallower going up, a pixel wider each side per step, levels 1 to `TOP_LEVEL`, and a whole stair for any run length); per step, lip / tread / shadow, toothed corners, the east face lit and the west shaded and both darker than the tread, a bed joint, at most one crack and never over the one below, each step lighter than the last; the wear, the coping; that a baked run *is* `flightPix` (a fixture run in a wall with rubble on one row); that no step is styled or drawn outside the flight; and the posts (the stair's stone, a lit top over a shaded face, the east one short, no glow).
- `tests/entrance.test.ts`: the hall and the flight as data and as built (flags, then steps, under rubble); earth where the foot stood and in the hall, none out on the flight; where the posts and cheeks stand; opening the stair clears hall and flight and leaves the stonework; the collision (hall wall to wall, posts in the corners, the footprint down each side and the treads between, the golem stopped against each cheek, and the walk from the Vestibule up and out); the v14 -> v15 migration (a clear body and everything else untouched, a dug-out v14 stair loading as hall and flight with no rock pile back on it, a body in each footprint set down a step aside and still in the passage, another area's bodies left alone, v13 running both steps).
- `tests/dungeonAudit.test.ts` names the new findings as what they are: the four pieces of stonework stand in solid tiles (rubble, until the stair is dug out) and the fall's boulders lie against them.

`scripts/rockPreview.mjs below` gained `stair-below-close.png`: the hall and flight at eight times, full-bright, with the golem in the hall and then walked as far into the west cheek as it will go.

**Left alone on purpose.** The stair head on the surface (it was the model), `stairfall`'s sprite, the rock piles and where they lie, the roots and stones round the mouth, the light down the stair. The `stairfoot` spot's id. A bone decal from the dungeon's own scatter that the foot used to hide now lies in the earth at the hall's mouth; it suits a fall, and moving it would mean touching the procedural pass.

### Post placement (v16)

Seen in play: the two newel posts looked as if they sank into the lowest step. A `stairpost` is a 12 x 19 sprite anchored at its feet, and it was anchored `post.d` (7) px south of the flight's north edge, so its bottom seven rows were drawn over the first tread (render it with `node scripts/rockPreview.mjs below`: `stair-below-close.png`).

- **The fix is data.** `STAIR_CHEEK.post.lift` (7) raises the post off the flight, so it is anchored at `sy + STAIR_CHEEK_TOP` (`sy` = the flight's north edge): its feet stand exactly on the edge, in the hall, and its base never touches a step. Seven is the overlap itself, so the post is flush with the flight, neither sunk nor floating. The sprite and its art are unchanged.
- **The footprints move with it.** A post's footprint (12 x 6, the same box) is now in the hall, and `staircheek`'s footprint begins where the post's ends: its box is `GATE_STAIR.h * TS - STAIR_CHEEK_TOP` deep (the whole flight), so there is no pocket between post and wall to stand in, and no overlap. The passage is as wide as before: 40 px stay open between the posts, and the hall is still wall to wall from the Vestibule down to them.
- **Why a migration.** The hall's last rows next to its walls are solid now, so a body saved standing there would be inside a post: `MIGRATIONS[15]` (see "Save format", v16). A body standing where a v15 post's base was, now open floor, is not moved.
- **Tests.** `tests/entrance.test.ts`: the posts' feet are on or north of the flight's edge, and `lift` stays within a few pixels; each cheek's footprint starts where its post's ends and runs to the top of the flight; the collision walk is as before; the v15 test now pins v15's own boxes and migrates to 15, and a new v16 test covers a body inside each new footprint (set down a step aside, in the passage), one in the hall against a wall, the bodies that must not move, another area, and a v14 save running both steps. The other tests that pin `SAVE_VERSION` moved to 16. No sprite, bake or golden hash changed.
